<h1 align="center">Launcher</h1>

<p align="center">
  A small, fast Android home screen where search is how you open things.<br>
  Material 3 Expressive, Material You colours, built for one hand.
</p>

## Features

- **Search first.** One search bar at the bottom finds apps and contacts as you type, and Enter opens
  the best match. Contacts come with WhatsApp and Call buttons, and you can search the web or the
  Play Store from the same bar.
- **A quiet home screen.** A clock, the date and a dock of up to four apps. Tap the clock to open
  your alarms, or the date to open today in your calendar.
- **App drawer.** Swipe up anywhere and it follows your finger. Swipe down for notifications.
- **Notification dots** on apps that have notifications, with no counts and no red.
- **Themed icons** in the dock and the drawer, the way Pixel does them.
- **Hidden apps**, kept out of the drawer and search.
- **Stay awake.** Long-press home to keep the screen on until you lock the phone.
- **Pebbles,** a wallpaper that switches with light and dark mode. You can also pick your own light and dark images.
- **Back up and restore** your settings to a file.
- **No internet access.** The launcher doesn't have the permission, so it can't track you or show ads.

Needs Android 13 or newer.

## Won't be added

These are left out on purpose, to keep the launcher fast, light and simple:

- Widgets
- App icons or shortcuts on the home screen, beyond the dock
- Folders
- More than one home page
- Icon packs
- A news feed or a Google Discover page
- App shortcuts in the long-press menu
- Notification counts on icons

## Install

Download the APK from the [latest release](https://github.com/SymphonySimper/launcher/releases/latest),
install it, then choose Launcher as your home app. Each release installs over the last one.

To build it yourself, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
