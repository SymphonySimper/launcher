# Privacy policy

Launcher, by SymphonySimper. Last updated 28 November 2026.

Launcher doesn't collect, store or share any of your data. It has no internet permission, no
accounts, no analytics and no ads, so nothing it sees can leave your phone through it.

## What it reads, and why

- **Contacts** (optional): names and phone numbers, so search can show matching contacts and let you
  call or message them. They're read only while you search and are never saved.
- **Notifications** (optional): only how many notifications each app has, to show badges on app
  icons. It doesn't read what the notifications say.
- **Installed apps**: to show them in the app drawer and dock.

You can turn contacts and badges off in the launcher's settings or in Android's settings at any time.

## Settings

Your launcher settings (dock, hidden apps and so on) are stored on your phone. If you use **Back up
settings**, they're saved to a file you choose. If Android backup is on, Android may include them in
your Google account's backup.

## Other apps

When you open a web search, a contact or another app, that app handles what happens next under its
own privacy policy.
